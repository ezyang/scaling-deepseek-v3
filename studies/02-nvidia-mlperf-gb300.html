Study 02 — NVIDIA MLPerf 6.0 submitted configuration (256 × GB300)

Part of a series of worked configurations. Study 01 covered a DSv3-family MoE on 2048 Hopper GPUs; this one covers NVIDIA's submitted 256-GPU GB300 configuration, which matches the published NeMo/Megatron-Bridge 26.06 recipe we use as our calibration anchor.

Configuration: 256 × GB300, TP1 · PP2 (×8 virtual stages) · EP32 · CP1, mbs 1, seq 4096, GBS 4096 sequences → dp 128, m = 32 microbatches/step. Published result: 6,338 tok/s/GPU = 1,648 model TFLOP/s/GPU = 10.30 s/step. Precision: MXFP8 (E4M3 + UE8M0 block scales) on every GEMM including the attention core — q/k/v are saved MXFP8, the attention output is saved bf16, rstd/lse fp32 — with fp8 parameter gather (fp8-only working weights), fp32 master weights and bf16 grads/Adam moments. No activation checkpointing. That is the headline of this study: with pp = 2 only two microbatches are ever in flight, and GB300's 288 GB absorbs the full stash-everything inventory with ~150 GB to spare.
